## The Economy Update
     -Cheaper weapons so you can buy them from the start
        *Nail gun $250 (was $600), shotgun $350 (was $750) and bow $400 (was $900)
        *Hunting rifle $650, blunderbuss $800 and assault rifle $1,000
        *The expensive guns come down too: gloop launcher $1,500, flamethrower $1,800, arc caster $2,300, PIR $2,800, minigun $3,200 and rail driver $4,500
        *Cheaper melee weapons: El Patron $250, jamón leg $400, axe $550, paella pan $700, cattle prod $850, capote $900, sledgehammer $1,000 and chainsaw $2,000

  ## Update 2.0
     -Larisa Update
        *Wally the horse and a bit more
        *You can only carry one gun and one melee weapon at a time (swap them in the stockroom)
        *Amulets are a bit rarer
        *Crates now have a health bar
        *Weaker green flames for the flamethrower
        *Weaker pets
        *Much pricier lunch box ($24,000)
        *The sledgehammer now sends out a shockwave
        *Thunderstorms drop lightning that can hit you or the zombies (move away when you see the blue glow on the ground)
        *Minigun slightly stronger
        *You start with just your pistol, the bat and 2 crates (no book or first-aid kit)
        *Bosses come every 5 shifts: weaker the first time you meet them, tougher every time they come back
        *Deep into a run, 2 or 3 bosses can show up in the same shift, one after another
        *New Base tab in the stockroom: roof turret, electric doors, cart launcher and freezer aisle
        *Flamethrower slightly weaker
        *Rail driver much weaker (much less damage, slower charge and it no longer goes through everything)
        *Chainsaw weaker
        *Far fewer zombies per shift later on (shift 60 goes from about 2,260 to about 300)
        *Each zombie pays more tips the further you get, to make up for it
        *Floor defenses are weaker: crates have less health, spikes and propane do less damage, and the turret shoots slower, hits softer and has less ammo
        *Spikes and turrets now have a health bar, and zombies stop to smash turrets
        *Hermana Sole is weaker: zombies lose half their health and bosses 7.5%, but she costs less ($1,300)
        *New weather: red moon (faster zombies but double tips), heatwave, meteor shower and desert snow
        *No more than 3 turrets on the road at once
        *Walking speed is capped at 120 (167% of normal)
        *Max health is capped at 250
