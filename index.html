Graveyard Shift

You work at the Alcampo out on desert Route 9, and every shift more of the dead come shuffling in off the dunes. Keep them away from you and the sliding doors until the shift ends, then spend your tips in the stockroom on guns, armor, pets and a few things nobody should sell.

How a shift works

  1. Before the doors open, place crates, spike strips, propane tanks and turrets on the road.
  2. They come in from the dunes on the right. Keep them away from you and the sliding doors.
  3. If you go down or the doors give in, the run is over and you start again from shift 1.
  4. When the shift ends you run back inside, collect tips plus a bonus, and restock. Defenses stay where you left them.
  5. Between shifts you can make 4 purchases. Upgrades don't count toward that, but each upgrade only goes up one level per buy phase.
  6. Melee weapons and the bow start very weak. The Strength upgrade makes them hit harder.
  7. Every fourth shift ends with a boss.

Where to shoot

  • Head. Double damage. Helmets and traffic cones block headshots completely.
  • Chest. Normal damage. Soldiers' vests cut it by 25%.
  • Legs. Normal damage, and it adds up. Put about a third of a zombie's health into its legs and they break. It drops to the ground, crawls at a third of its speed, and any helmet or cone falls off. Bosses cannot be crippled.

Weather

  • Clear night. Dark past the lamps. Your flashlight follows your aim.
  • Scorching day. Full visibility. Tumbleweeds.
  • Thunderstorm. Dark and wet. Fire burns half as long, and lightning sometimes strikes one of them.
  • Full moon. Rare (1 shift in 20), from shift 3. You turn into a werewolf: 35% faster, double melee damage, 20% less damage taken. A flock of pigeons pecks at the zombies all night.
  • Sandstorm. You only see what is near you. Everything walks 15% slower.

The stockroom shows the forecast for the next shift.
